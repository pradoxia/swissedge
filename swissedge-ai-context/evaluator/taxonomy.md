## Purpose

Define safe, high-level situation categories for SwissEdge research routing.

## Situation Families

- Merger: corporate transaction or proposed business combination.
- Merger arbitrage: transaction path where deal completion terms, timing, financing, approvals, and spread drivers matter.
- Spin-off: separation of a business into an independent public company or related distribution.
- Tender offer: issuer or third-party offer to purchase shares under defined terms.
- Bankruptcy / liquidation: restructuring, liquidation, dissolution, or distressed process.
- Proxy fight / activism: governance contest or activist campaign.
- Rights offering: shareholder subscription rights or capital raise structure.
- Unknown / out of scope: insufficient metadata or unsupported situation.

## Safety

Do not infer final investment merit from taxonomy alone. Taxonomy only supports routing and triage.
