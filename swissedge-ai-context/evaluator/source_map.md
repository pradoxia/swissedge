## Source Categories

- Official primary: regulator filings, company filings, court documents, exchange documents.
- Official secondary: company investor relations pages, official press releases, transaction microsites.
- Trusted external: reputable publications or curated professional feeds.
- External unverified: social posts, newsletters, rumors, informal alerts.
- Manual input: Dani-provided note, paste, or Telegram-style intake.

## Rule

External signals can start research but should not be treated as proof. Official-source verification is required before deep research.
