# SwissEdge Publication Methodology

## Purpose

SwissEdge publication artifacts are educational research outputs derived from documented ResearchCases. They are not investment advice.

## Requirements

- Public drafts only after a ResearchCase is documented.
- Methodology must be course-grounded through processed artifacts.
- Evidence must be traceable to stored metadata and approved excerpts/snippets.
- Missing information and risks must be stated clearly.
- No private infrastructure, private notes, internal IDs, secrets, or sensitive metadata.

## Prohibited

- Buy/sell/hold recommendation language.
- Auto-publishing.
- Private production details.
- Raw course transcripts or copyrighted course text.
- Unsupported claims.

## Disclaimer

Every public-facing draft must include educational-only disclaimer language.
