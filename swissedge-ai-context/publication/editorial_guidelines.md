# SwissEdge Editorial Guidelines

## Voice

Clear, cautious, evidence-led, and educational.

## Safety

- No financial advice.
- No buy/sell/hold recommendation language.
- No urgency language that implies action.
- No private metadata or operational details.
- No raw course text.

## Structure

- What happened.
- Why it may matter.
- Evidence and source status.
- Methodology frame.
- Key unknowns.
- Risks.
- Next research questions.
- Disclaimer.

## Review

Dani manually reviews every draft before any external use. AI may draft and flag issues but does not approve or publish.
