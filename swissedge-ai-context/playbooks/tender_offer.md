## Purpose

Identify tender offer situations and structure verification around offer terms, deadlines, conditions, and official documentation.

## Situation Type

Tender offer, issuer tender, third-party tender, going-private tender, exchange offer, or related SEC Schedule TO signal.

## Evidence Needed

- Official Schedule TO, offer document, or company announcement.
- Offer price or exchange terms.
- Expiration date and extension rules.
- Proration, minimum condition, financing, regulatory, and withdrawal terms.

## Methodology Status

Partial / human review required unless processed core artifacts confirm evaluator readiness for the specific tender structure.

## Key Risks

- Conditional or amended offer terms.
- Proration.
- Withdrawal rights.
- Financing or regulatory uncertainty.
- Expiration or extension timing.
- Confusing issuer vs third-party mechanics.

## Human Review Notes

Human review should confirm official documents, current deadlines, and whether the tender is complete enough for deeper research.
