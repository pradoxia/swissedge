# Merger - AI-Safe Summary

AI-safe summary placeholder. Do not treat this as raw course content. Validate against processed core artifacts before implementation.

## Purpose

Identify merger-related situations and decide whether they require merger arbitrage analysis, monitoring, or archive.

## Situation Type

Merger, acquisition, definitive merger proxy, preliminary transaction signal, strategic alternatives, or non-binding offer.

## Evidence Needed

- Official filing or company announcement.
- Transaction terms and consideration.
- Required approvals and expected timeline.
- Financing, regulatory, litigation, termination, and closing-condition details.

## Methodology Status

Higher maturity when official primary evidence and core transaction terms are present. Methodology status still requires confirmation against processed core artifacts before automation.

## Key Risks

- Non-binding or incomplete terms.
- Financing uncertainty.
- Regulatory or shareholder approval risk.
- Litigation.
- Ambiguous consideration or missing timeline.

## Human Review Notes

Human review should decide whether the case belongs in deep research, monitoring, or archive. Do not infer investment action from the presence of a merger signal.

## Safe Usage

Use for routing, evidence planning, and research task generation only. Do not use as standalone financial analysis.

## Disclaimer

Educational research only. Not financial advice. No buy/sell/hold recommendation language.
