# Bankruptcy - AI-Safe Summary

AI-safe summary placeholder. Do not treat this as raw course content. Validate against processed core artifacts before implementation.

## Purpose

Identify bankruptcy, restructuring, or distress situations and require careful evidence review before any deeper analysis.

## Situation Type

Bankruptcy, restructuring, Chapter 11, debt exchange, distressed recapitalization, liquidation, or related court/filing signal.

## Evidence Needed

- Official court filing, company filing, or company announcement.
- Capital structure and creditor classes.
- Key court dates, plan milestones, debtor-in-possession financing, and restructuring support agreements when available.
- Evidence of plan status, claims, and recovery mechanics.

## Methodology Status

Partial / human review required. Bankruptcy cases are complex and should remain conservative unless processed core artifacts define a validated evaluator path.

## Key Risks

- Incomplete capital structure.
- Court process uncertainty.
- Creditor priority complexity.
- Recovery assumptions.
- Stale or unofficial source information.
- High false-positive risk from generic distress language.

## Human Review Notes

Human review is mandatory before deeper research. Confirm official sources, current docket status, and whether the case is in scope.

## Safe Usage

Use only for triage, evidence planning, and risk labeling. Do not produce investment conclusions or recommendations.

## Disclaimer

Educational research only. Not financial advice. No buy/sell/hold recommendation language.
