# Merger Arbitrage - AI-Safe Summary

AI-safe summary placeholder. Do not treat this as raw course content. Validate against processed core artifacts before implementation.

## Purpose

Assess announced transaction situations where deal completion probability, timing, and key conditions drive the research workflow.

## Situation Type

Merger arbitrage, cash merger, stock-for-stock merger, acquisition tender, or definitive deal signal routed from a merger playbook.

## Evidence Needed

- Official primary filing or transaction announcement.
- Deal consideration and structure.
- Approval requirements.
- Financing status.
- Regulatory conditions.
- Expected closing timeline and termination terms.

## Methodology Status

Evaluator-ready or higher maturity may be appropriate when official primary evidence and all core transaction terms are available. Confirm exact readiness against processed core artifacts before implementation.

## Key Risks

- Regulatory block.
- Financing failure.
- Shareholder rejection.
- Litigation.
- Timeline slippage.
- Hidden conditionality not visible in summary metadata.

## Human Review Notes

Human review must validate terms, evidence quality, and whether the case should move to deep research or monitoring. No automated recommendation is allowed.

## Safe Usage

Use to structure research tasks and evidence checks. Do not produce price targets, trade instructions, or buy/sell/hold language.

## Disclaimer

Educational research only. Not financial advice. No buy/sell/hold recommendation language.
